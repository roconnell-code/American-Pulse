About the desk

Every state. Every story.

American Pulse is a seven-day reading room for the United States. Choose a state to see reporting that names that place, then open the original article at the publisher.

Window

Only items with a publication time in the previous seven days stay on the desk. Older wire copy is discarded.

Sources

Publisher feeds from The New York Times, NPR, BBC, and The Washington Post. State and search results also use the Bing News index for local desks, including Associated Press and Reuters when they appear there. An optional Guardian or New York Times API key can be set in the server environment.

Summaries

A summary is the publisher’s standfirst or the snippet supplied with the result. This desk does not write articles, rewrite headlines, or fill gaps with estimated numbers.

When the wire is quiet

If a feed does not respond, the panel says news is unavailable and shows nothing in its place. An empty state means no matching article cleared the date and relevance checks.
